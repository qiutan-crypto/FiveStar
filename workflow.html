Five Star Tax Resolution – Operational Workflow

From lead → intake → tax analysis → IRS submission → post-resolution

1. Lead & Intake
2. Case Setup
3. Tax Analysis
4. IRS Submission
5. Resolution & Follow-up
1. Client Acquisition & Intake Sales · Marketing · Openers · Customer Service

Departments:

  • Marketing (Amelia Downes), Technical Ops (Chase Parker)
  • Opening Team (Matthew Kim, Ryan Kim, Justin Huerta, Wessam Ibrahim)
  • Sales Team (Ashley Park, Brandon Jones, Ryan Harmon, etc.)
  • Customer Service (Johnniessa “JoJo” Everett)

Steps:

  • Run ads / referrals → leads enter CRM auto
  • Openers call, verify tax problem, collect basics
  • Sales explains programs, secures engagement letter & payment
  • Account created → handed to Case Management

Output: “New Active Case” with client data + signed authorization.

2. Case Setup & IRS Investigation Case Workers · Case Managers · Office Admin

Departments:

  • Case Workers (Jeff Tong, Shyenne Perkins, Christian Villanueva, etc.)
  • Case Managers / Supervisors (Al Frias, Cheryl McManus, Rosa Lopez)
  • Office Admin (Mia Luna)

Steps:

  • Case Manager assigns case to a Case Worker
  • Collect IRS letters, prior returns, IDs from client
  • File POA (Form 2848) so Five Star can talk to IRS
  • Pull IRS transcripts & balances; log in system

Output: “Investigated Case” with IRS data ready for tax strategy.

3. Tax Analysis & Strategy Tax Managers · Attorneys · CPA/EA

Departments:

  • Tax Managers (Mark Pineda, Trevon Ross)
  • Tax Attorneys (Randy Salter, Victor Latham, Edward Korducki)
  • CPA / EA (Sarah Gao, Albert Leary)
  • Tax Assistants (Alston Zhou, Daniel Ferrumpau-Perez)

Steps:

  • Review transcripts & compliance status
  • Prepare financials (Form 433) and ability-to-pay analysis
  • Choose program: Offer in Compromise, IA, CNC, Penalty Abatement
  • Present plan to client → get approval & signatures

Output: “Approved Resolution Plan” + supporting financial docs.

4. IRS Submission & Negotiation Tax Dept · Case Workers

Departments:

  • Tax Department (attorneys, EA, CPA)
  • Case Workers for follow-up calls

Steps:

  • Submit full package to IRS / state
  • Track deadlines, respond to IRS additional info requests
  • Attorney/EA negotiates amount or terms with IRS officer
  • Record acceptance letter / agreement in client file

Output: “Resolved Case” – accepted OIC, IA set up, or CNC approved.

5. Resolution & Follow-up Accounting · Customer Service · Marketing

Departments:

  • Accounting / Bookkeeping (Steven Krause, Lifeng Liu)
  • Customer Service / Complaints (Destinie Langford)
  • Marketing (for review / retention)

Steps:

  • Record IRS acceptance / finalized agreement in client file
  • Finance tracks installment or OIC lump-sum payments
  • Customer Service calls client to confirm issue is resolved
  • Send review link (Google / BBB) to happy clients
  • Add client to annual tax / bookkeeping follow-up list

Output: “Closed & retained client” — case archived, client stays in nurture cycle.

Tip: you can plug this into your internal portal and map each step to your real people (the 63 from the directory) so managers can see workloads.
